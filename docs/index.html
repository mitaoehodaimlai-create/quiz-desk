Quiz Desk

A proctored MCQ quiz platform for a class. Faculty upload a student list and a question bank, run timed quizzes, and see results as students submit.

Open the demo Source on GitHub

The demo runs entirely in your browser. Uploads, quizzes and answers stay in that tab and are cleared when you reload. Real classes use the claude.ai version, where results are stored and shared between faculty and students.

Upload

Student lists and question banks from Excel or CSV, matched by column header.

Proctor

Tab switches, copy, paste and content from other sites cost marks, with auto-submit after repeated violations.

Results

Live ranking, accuracy per question, violations, and CSV export.

Try it in two minutes

  1. Open the demo and set any faculty password.
  2. On Students and Question bank, press the sample buttons, then Save.
  3. On Quizzes, give it a title, press Create quiz, then Go live.
  4. Press Student view, enter roll number AIML-A-01, and take the quiz.
  5. Back in the faculty view, open Results.
