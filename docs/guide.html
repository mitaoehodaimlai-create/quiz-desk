User guide

How to use Quiz Desk

From first setup to results, for administrators, faculty and students. Each group of up to 30 students has its own link with its own students, questions and results.

Administrator · once per group

1. Set up the group

  1. Open your group's link

    Sign in to claude.ai first, then open the Quiz Desk link for the group.

  2. Create the administrator account

    Enter your name, a username and a password of 8 or more characters, then press Create account and log in. Do this straight away: whoever opens a new group first becomes its administrator.

  3. Add faculty accounts

    Press Accounts. For each colleague, enter a name, username and temporary password, choose Faculty or Administrator, and press Add faculty account. Give each person their username and password privately.

  4. Share the page

    In claude.ai, open the page's Share menu.

    • Faculty: Editor access.
    • That group's students only: Contributor access.

  5. Know the two links

    Each group has a faculty link (the page's address) and a student link (the same address ending in #student). The student link always opens the quiz screen for that group, never the faculty login. Log in and copy it from the card at the top of the Quizzes tab.

Faculty

2. Prepare a quiz

  1. Log in

    Open the group link and log in with your username and password. The first time, open My account and change your temporary password.

  2. Upload the student list

    On Students, drop an Excel or CSV file whose first row has the headers Roll No, Name, Email, Division (Email and Division are optional). Check the preview and press Save. Keep each group to 30 students.

  3. Upload the question bank

    On Question bank, drop a file with the headers Question, A, B, C, D, Answer, Topic, Difficulty, Marks. The answer can be A–D, 1–4 or the option's text. Line breaks inside a question are kept, so code snippets display correctly. Check the preview and press Save.

  4. Create the quiz

    On Quizzes:

    • Enter a title, the number of questions, minutes, difficulty and topics, and the divisions it's for.
    • Set proctoring: marks cut per violation, free warnings, and auto-submit after a number of violations.
    • Press Create quiz. It starts as a Draft, which students can't see.
Faculty · during the exam

3. Run the quiz

  1. Go live

    When students are ready, press Go live on the quiz card. Your name is recorded as the person who started it.

  2. Send students their group's student link

    On Quizzes, press Copy student link and send it to that group only. Keep the faculty link among faculty. Students follow the steps in the next section.

  3. Watch progress

    The Results tab shows who is answering now, who has submitted, tab switches and other violations. In-progress scores can trail by about 15–20 seconds.

  4. Close

    When time is up, press Close. Anyone still answering is submitted automatically.

Students

Taking a quiz

  1. Sign in and open the link

    Sign in to claude.ai with your college account and open the student link your teacher sent for your group (it ends in #student). Use only your own group's link.

  2. Enter your roll number

    Type it exactly as it appears on the class list. It's tied to your account after your first quiz.

  3. Read the rules and start

    Press Start quiz, read the rules, then I agree, start quiz. The timer starts now and keeps running if you close the page.

  4. Answer

    Click an option, or press A–D. Use the answer sheet to jump between questions and Mark for review to come back later. Answers save automatically.

  5. Submit

    Press Submit quiz and confirm. When your teacher releases results, open the quiz again to see your score and the correct answers.

Stay on the quiz tab. Switching tabs or windows, copying, pasting, right-clicking, bringing in content from other websites, or leaving full screen is recorded and can cost marks. Repeated violations submit your quiz automatically.

Faculty · after the exam

4. Results and analytics

  1. Review results

    On Results: scores after deductions, tab switches with time away, accuracy for each question with its full text, and who didn't start. Press Export CSV to download.

  2. Find slow and advanced learners

    On Learners, choose one quiz or the average of all quizzes, and adjust the thresholds if needed (defaults: slow below 40%, advanced from 75%). Review weak topics for each group and press Export CSV for NAAC/NBA records. Levels use marks before proctoring deductions.

  3. Release results

    Press Release results on the quiz card. Students then see their score, any deductions and the correct answers.

  4. Save everything

    On Results or Learners, press Save all results (Excel). One workbook for the group holds a summary, a results sheet for every quiz, question analysis with full questions, learner levels, topic accuracy by level, every violation, the student list and the activity log. Save it after each quiz and keep it in your records.

  5. Check activity and log out

    Activity shows who did what and when. Press Log out when you finish.
